About the artist

Made for people who care about what things mean

Luca Bermejo is a graphic designer and artist working across visual identity, editorial, and art direction. His practice is shaped by a way of thinking that treats design as a compositional language: precise, atmospheric, and built around ideas rather than aesthetics alone. Co-Founder of KIDZ and Equinox Multimedia. He works with cultural institutions, independent labels and artists, publishers, and brands.
